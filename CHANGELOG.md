# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-08)

First release.

- Reminders with their own intervals (2 seconds to 24 hours), templates for blink, water, eye rest, posture, stretch and breathing, and custom icon reminders.
- Animated on-screen scenes drawn in a transparent, click-through window.
- Synthesized sounds per reminder.
- Pause for 15 minutes, 30 minutes, an hour or the rest of the day.
- Active hours and days, including windows past midnight.
- Smart pause: away from the computer, locked screen and full-screen apps (Windows).
- System tray menu, start with your computer, light and dark themes, reduced-motion support.
- Everything stays on the device: no network access, accounts or analytics.
- Available as a Windows installer, from the Microsoft Store, and as a Linux AppImage.
